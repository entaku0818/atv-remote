Purpose and audience
Pocket Remote is a consumer utility for owners of compatible Google TV / Android TV devices who want to use an iPhone or iPad as an additional remote. It provides directional navigation, OK, Back, Home, volume, mute, power commands, and sending a URL to a compatible app on the TV. It does not provide or stream video content.

Setup and main features
1. Turn on the TV using its physical controls or original remote. Complete its network setup first.
2. Connect the iPhone/iPad and TV to the same local network. The TV must support Android TV Remote Service. Guest networks or network isolation can prevent communication.
3. Launch Pocket Remote and allow Local Network access.
4. Select the discovered TV, or enter its IP address from the TV's network settings.
5. Enter the six-character pairing code shown on the TV.
6. Use directional buttons and OK to navigate, then Back and Home. Volume and mute depend on the TV/audio setup.
7. Optionally enter a URL supported by an app installed on the TV and tap “TVで開く”. The TV app determines URL support; not every URL can be played.
Power behavior depends on the TV. A TV that disconnects from the network when off cannot be discovered or powered on by this app. This app cannot perform an offline TV's initial network setup.
Settings, connection help, privacy information, and IP entry can be viewed without a TV. Core remote functionality requires a compatible physical TV on the same network.

Accounts, payment, and user content
No registration, login, or user accounts exist, so account deletion and demo credentials are not applicable. No sample files are required. There is no community, messaging, content feed, or user-content publishing. Entered URLs are sent only to the user's TV, not published to other users.
This is a paid app download. All features are available after installation. There are no in-app purchases, subscriptions, paywalls, or feature unlocks.

External services, tools, and platforms
- Google TV / Android TV with Android TV Remote Service.
- Bonjour/mDNS discovery; local TLS pairing and remote connections to TV ports 6467 and 6466.
- Apple Network, Security, and CryptoKit frameworks; bundled SwiftProtobuf open-source protocol library.
- No developer-operated backend, cloud authentication, ads, analytics SDK, or AI service. RevenueCat is not integrated.
- Apple handles the paid download through the App Store.
- URL opening depends on installed TV apps and their separate accounts/subscriptions. Pocket Remote does not provide subscriptions or bypass access restrictions.

Regional behavior
The app's own functionality is identical across regions, with no regional feature gates or content catalog. The interface is Japanese. TV apps, URL support, and third-party content availability may differ by device, provider, and region. Prices vary by storefront.

Regulated services and third-party material
This is a remote-control utility, not a highly regulated service. It does not bundle, host, or distribute third-party videos, music, or images, bypass DRM, or grant access to protected content. Google TV / Android TV references describe compatibility, not affiliation with Google or TV manufacturers.
