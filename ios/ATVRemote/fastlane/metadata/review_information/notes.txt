Purpose and intended audience
Pocket Remote is a consumer utility for people who own a compatible Google TV / Android TV and want to use an iPhone or iPad as an additional remote control. It provides directional navigation, OK, Back, Home, volume, mute, power commands, and sending a URL to a compatible app installed on the TV. It does not provide or stream video content.

Setup and main user flow
1. Use the TV's physical controls or original remote to turn on the TV. Complete the TV's network setup first.
2. Connect the iPhone/iPad and TV to the same local network. The TV must support Android TV Remote Service. Guest networks or network isolation can prevent communication.
3. Launch Pocket Remote and allow Local Network access when prompted.
4. Select the discovered TV. If discovery is unavailable, enter the TV's IP address from its network settings.
5. Enter the six-character pairing code shown on the TV.
6. Use the directional buttons and OK to navigate the TV, then Back and Home. Test volume and mute where supported by the TV/audio setup.
7. Optionally enter a URL supported by an app installed on the TV and tap the button to open it on the TV. The TV app determines whether the URL can be opened; playback of every URL is not guaranteed.
Power commands depend on the TV. A TV that disconnects from the network when powered off cannot be discovered or powered on by this app. This app cannot perform the initial network setup of an offline TV.
Settings, the connection guide, privacy information, and manual IP entry are available without a TV. Reviewing the remote-control functionality requires a compatible physical TV on the same network.

Accounts, paid features, and user content
There is no account registration, login, or account deletion flow because this app has no user accounts. No demo credentials or sample files are required. There is no user-content publishing, sharing community, messaging, or content feed. A user-entered URL is sent to the user's TV; it is not published to other users.
The app is a paid download. All features are available immediately after installation. There are no in-app purchases, subscriptions, paywalls, or additional feature unlocks.

External services, tools, and platforms
- A compatible Google TV / Android TV running Android TV Remote Service is required for the core remote functionality.
- Discovery uses Bonjour/mDNS on the local network. Pairing and remote commands use local TLS connections to the TV (ports 6467 and 6466 respectively).
- Apple Network, Security, and CryptoKit frameworks provide networking and cryptographic operations. SwiftProtobuf is a bundled open-source library for protocol messages, not a hosted service.
- There is no developer-operated backend, cloud authentication, advertising SDK, analytics SDK, or AI service. RevenueCat is not integrated into the submitted app.
- Apple handles the paid app download through the App Store.
- Opening a URL depends on apps installed on the TV and any separate accounts/subscriptions those apps require. Pocket Remote does not provide those subscriptions or bypass access restrictions.

Regional behavior
Pocket Remote's own functionality is the same in all regions; it does not apply region-specific feature gates or provide a regional content catalog. Its interface is currently Japanese. Compatible TV apps, their URL handling, and content availability can differ by device, provider, and region. App Store prices vary by storefront.

Regulated services and third-party material
The app is a remote-control utility, not a service in a highly regulated industry. It does not bundle, host, license, or distribute third-party video, music, or images. It does not bypass DRM or provide access to protected content. Google TV / Android TV references describe compatibility; the app does not claim affiliation with Google or TV manufacturers.
